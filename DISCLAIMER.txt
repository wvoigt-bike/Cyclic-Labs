CYCLIC LABS - SIMULATION DISCLAIMER
===================================

Please read this before using the strength simulation (the "Strength (FEA)"
tab: stress, strain, fatigue life, ISO 4210 lab tests, stiffness, comfort,
weld joints and the CalculiX check).

IT'S A SIMULATION, NOT AN APPROVAL
  Cyclic Labs is an independent hobby tool for sketching and comparing bike
  frame designs. Its strength results are rough estimates from a simplified
  computer model. They are NOT engineering advice, a design approval or a
  safety certification.

THERE MAY BE MISTAKES
  The results can be wrong: there may be bugs in the software, the physics is
  simplified, and the material strength, weld quality, riding loads and crash
  forces are all assumptions. Numbers typed in, imported or read off a drawing
  by the scanner can be wrong too. The tool has not been checked by a licensed
  engineer or proven against real lab tests of your frame.

A PASS DOESN'T MEAN SAFE, AND A FAIL DOESN'T MEAN UNSAFE
  Only a real frame on a real test rig passes or fails ISO 4210. Use the
  numbers to compare one design with another and to come up with questions,
  not as verdicts.

ALWAYS ASK YOUR FRAME BUILDER OR A QUALIFIED FRAME DESIGNER
  Have them review and approve every design before it is made, and follow
  their advice over anything Cyclic Labs says. Your frame builder is
  responsible for the final design, materials, welding and testing.

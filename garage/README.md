# Garage bikes

Bikes you can load into Cyclic Labs: open the **Garage** page, choose **Import**, and pick a `.json` file from this folder.
They're saved in your browser after that, like any bike you make. Their parts are in `app/my_parts.js`.

- **John's 32'er**: a friend's 32-inch drop-bar bike, built from his frame builder's build sheet. Numbers the sheet doesn't
  give (fork, tube sizes, material) are assumed; the bike's notes say which.
